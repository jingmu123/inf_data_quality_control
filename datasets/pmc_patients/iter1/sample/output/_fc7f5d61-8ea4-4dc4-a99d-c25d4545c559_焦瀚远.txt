【0】Unusual Presentation of a Rare Tumor of the Dorsal Surface of the Foot
A 60-year-old woman in good general condition with no history of any trauma exhibited a palpable mass on the dorsal surface of the right foot. The lesion slowly enlarged over 12 years.
