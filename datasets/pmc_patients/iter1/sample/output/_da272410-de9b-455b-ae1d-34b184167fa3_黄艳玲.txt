【0】Prevalence of CYP17A1 gene mutations in 17α-hydroxylase deficiency in the Chinese Han population
Patient 5 was a 13-year-old girl who was hospitalized because of high blood pressure lasting for 2 months. Her average daytime blood pressure was 187/127 mmHg, and her heart rate was 76 bpm. Blood potassium was extremely low, with a value of 2.7 mmol/l. She had a normal BMI but immature breasts and vulva and had not experienced menstruation. Her plasma E2, TESTO and renin activity were all decreased, whereas PROG, FSH, LH, aldosterone and ARR were increased . She had reduced blood cortisol and increased ACTH. Pelvic MRI showed an immature uterus and ovaries, and her chromosome karyotype was 46XX.黄艳玲
