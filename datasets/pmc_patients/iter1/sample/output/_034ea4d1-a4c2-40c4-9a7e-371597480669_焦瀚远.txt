【0】Smooth borders between inner nuclear layer and outer plexiform layer predict fewer macular edema recurrences in branch retinal vein occlusion
Case 1 was a 77-year-old woman who presented with visual loss due to ME secondary to BRVO in the left eye. At the initial visit, ME was observed on OCT images, which showed large cystoid spaces in the OPL and a small amount of subretinal fluid . The border between the INL and OPL was relatively smooth , and the JR was 1.01. After one anti-VEGF injection at the initial visit, ME was not observed until the end of the 12-month study period.焦瀚远
