【0】Medial and Lateral Canthal Reconstruction with an Orbicularis Oculi Myocutaneous Island Flap
A 59-year-old man presented with a biopsy-proven basal cell carcinoma involving the left lower eyelid and medial canthus. The carcinoma had first occurred 1 year ago, in the form of a pigmented papule. The patient underwent excision with a 3-mm margin, followed by reconstruction with a 4-cm-long and 6-mm-wide, medially based orbicularis oculi myocutaneous island flap from the left upper eyelid. The flap was transferred to the site of the defect (measuring 1×0.9 cm) through a subdermal tunnel in the medial canthal area after partial deepithelialization. The punctum was found to be involved, and an ophthalmologist subsequently performed lacrimal duct reconstruction, using a silicone tube.
