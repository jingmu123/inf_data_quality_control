【0】Case Report on a Rare Intraoperative Finding of Ectopic Liver Tissue Attached to Gallbladder Wall during Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy
The patient was a 42-year-old female with a known case of asthma. She had recurrent episodes of upper abdominal pain referred to back and right shoulder which was associated with fatty meals. Ultrasound showed multiple gall stones. She was admitted for elective laparoscopic cholecystectomy on January 8th 2019.
