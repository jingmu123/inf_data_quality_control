【0】An Anthropometric and Three-Dimensional Computed Tomographic Evaluation of Two-Point Fixation of Zygomatic Complex Fractures
A 32-year-old woman who experienced a zygomatic fracture on the left side underwent two-point fixation. The preoperative CT image indicated inferior and posterior dislocation of the zygoma body along with small zygomaticofrontal suture diastasis.
