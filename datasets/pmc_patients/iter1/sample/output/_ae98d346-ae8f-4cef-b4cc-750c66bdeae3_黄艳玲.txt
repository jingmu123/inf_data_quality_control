Here, we report the case of a 67-year-old patient, male, who underwent coronary artery bypass grafting at 54 years of age after a diagnosis of myocardial infarction. An angioplasty and 13 coronary angiography tests were performed. In 1992, he underwent nephrectomy for a malignant tumor in the right kidney. Scintigraphy performed in 2014 showed moderate and transient hypocaptation (ischemia) in the anteroseptal middle and basal region of the left ventricle. The patient was then enrolled in the study “Autologous transplantation of mesenchymal cells for treatment of severe and refractory ischemic cardiomyopathy” (Local Ethics Committee number: 5250). His bone marrow mononuclear cells were collected and isolated by performing density gradient centrifugation and were used as a source of MSCs.
