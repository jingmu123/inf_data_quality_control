【0】The Clinical Understaging of Recurrent Glottic Carcinoma after Radiation Failure
A 47-year-old male was treated with a salvage vertical partial hemilaryngectomy after failed radiation for an early stage (cT1a) squamous cell carcinoma of the glottis.
