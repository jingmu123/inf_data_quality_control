【0】Ultrabiomicroscopy Anterior Segment Evaluation of Ocular Contusive Trauma Caused by Pressurized Bottled Drink Caps: A Case Report
We report the case of a 69-year-old male, who was hit by a cork while he was opening a sparkling wine bottle. The subject presented to Policlinico Umberto I First Aid, where he underwent medical examinations.
He had a BCVA (best corrected visual acuity) of 20/100 and ecchymosis of the superior and inferior eyelid. Slit-lamp examination showed conjunctival and pericorneal hyperemia, Descemet's folds, anterior chamber of normal dimension, cellular reaction (Tyndall 3+), a big clot from h6 to h10 hanging in the anterior chamber and partially obscuring the pupil, lens in situ.
The fundus was difficult to explore due to vitreous turbidity, even though the optic disc appeared pink with a sharp margin and Berlin's edema was detected. We decided to perform an ultrabiomicroscopy (UBM) (50MHz) evaluation to improve corneal damage. Thanks to that, we understood the importance of UMB exam as a diagnostic instrumental exam which supports biomicroscopy in anterior segment study.
