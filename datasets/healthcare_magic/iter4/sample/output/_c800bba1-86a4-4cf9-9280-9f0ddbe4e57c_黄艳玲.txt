【3】**Answer**: Brief Answer:Either pityriasis rosea or sarcoidosis or granuloma annulare Detailed Answer:According to your complaints there are few possibilities:either pityriasis rosea which is an allergic reaction to drugs and food or sarcoidosis or granuloma annulare.If I was her treating doctor I would recommend her to take tablet aciclovir 800 mg four times in a day for 5 days and apply cortisone cream diluted in a moisturising lotion in the ratio of 1:3 twice daily for 7-10 days.Oral aciclovir is a prescription drug and you need to see the doctor in person to get them.If the rash doesn't improve in a week's time go for skin biopsy of lesions to find out its exact cause.黄艳玲
