【0】Why Does My Heart Rate Fluctuate To The Extent That It Dips Quite Low?
**Question**: My resting heart rate dips into the low 50’s and even mid 40’s for very short periods of time. I am not particularly fit, I am 56 yo female, this happens while I am awake. This happens daily but accumulated for less than 5-7 minutes per day. Is this anything to worry about?
