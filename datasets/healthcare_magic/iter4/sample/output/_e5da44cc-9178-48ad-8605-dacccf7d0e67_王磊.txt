Vomiting and felling ill and weak is due to migraine it self.Please discuss with your doctor and try to look for some regular medicines.Apart from this, migraine normally needs a trigger, means an action or cause which triggers the attack of migraine.With good observation and keeping a diary of events, will help you identify that.
