【0】Family Vacation, Periods Due To Start Then, Not On Birth Control, Medication To Delay Periods
**Question**: Hi, my family and I are going on vacation for one week in just a few weeks, I am scheduled to start my period that same week. I was wondering if there s anything that can be done to delay it. I am not on any birth control . I am 32 and my periods have always been normal and pretty much on time. Can you help? Thank you so much for your time! 

【1】**Answer**:删除10:<u> General & Family Physician,  Dr. Pavan Kumar Gupta's  Response</u> Hello. You can take preparation of medroxyprogesterone by the name of MEPRATE 10 mg daily till you want to postpone your periods. But this medicine has to be started at least 3 to 4 days prior to starting of your periods date,otherwise the effect won't come.删除1:<u> Thanks</u>焦瀚远
